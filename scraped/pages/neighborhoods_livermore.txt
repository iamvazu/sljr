Welcome to Livermore 
Home of the Cowboys and Granada Matadors. 
Livermore is home to a flourishing wine country and is quickly making a name for itself with award-winning wines and genuine hospitality. Whether it’s a small family-owned operation or one of the historic wineries, guests will be able to feel a neighborly ambiance while being treated as an old friend. Stroll downtown in the beautiful, pedestrian amenity rich streets and explore some of the 100+ retail stores, restaurants, wine bars, craft beer tap rooms, theaters, public parks and sports complexes. Golf courses include Las Positas Poppy Ridge and Wente Vineyards.
