Agent Details 
Steven Lenz Jr. is a premier full-time REALTOR® and Luxury Real Estate Advisor specializing in the East Bay’s prestigious Tri-Valley and Diablo Valley regions across Contra Costa and Alameda Counties. 
As a lifelong Tri-Valley native raised in Pleasanton and an alumnus of Amador Valley High School, Steven offers clients an unparalleled, foundational understanding of the local market landscape. His deep regional roots and extensive civic network provide buyers and sellers with a distinct competitive advantage in the fast-paced Bay Area market. 
Professional Experience & Philosophy 
Prior to his career in real estate, Steven competed as a semi-professional basketball player throughout the Bay Area. He seamlessly translated the elite discipline, teamwork, and work ethic forged on the court into a stellar corporate reputation. A dedicated educator of the game, Steven spent nine years coaching at De La Salle High School and currently coaches at the collegiate level for Ohlone College. He channels this extensive coaching background into his real estate practice, expertly guiding and educating clients through the complexities of the home-buying and selling processes. 
Strategic Advantages 
Market Expertise: Specialized knowledge of master-planned communities, top-tier school districts, and shifting market dynamics across Pleasanton, Walnut Creek, and surrounding valleys. 
Masterful Negotiation: Proven track record of securing optimal terms for clients, backed by clear, proactive communication. 
Technological Proficiency: Utilization of cutting-edge real estate technology, data analytics, and digital marketing strategies to maximize property exposure and streamline transactions. 
White-Glove Service: A client-centric approach that ensures a seamless, transparent experience for first-time homebuyers and seasoned investors alike. 
Whether managing a high-end luxury listing or securing a foundational property, Steven Lenz Jr. delivers an exceptional standard of service defined by integrity, dedication, and elite results. 

Sports Details 

While growing up in Pleasanton, Steven played basketball throughout the Bay Area and moved up the ranks to play semi-pro. For the past 10 years, Steven has coached basketball at the high school level at De La Salle High School in Concord, California. His experience with basketball has taught him the importance of hard work, dedication, and teamwork. Steven is currently working to focus his real estate business to the sports entertainment industry.
