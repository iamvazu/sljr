Meet Steven 
Realtor® 
Steven Lenz Jr. is a full-time REALTOR®️ specializing in the East Bay’s Tri-Valley communities located in Contra Costa and Alameda Counties. 
Steven is uniquely qualified to help buyers and sellers in these areas because he is a Tri-Valley native. He was raised and attended school in Pleasanton and is a proud graduate of Amador Valley High School. While growing up in Pleasanton, Steven played basketball throughout the Bay Area and moved up the ranks to play semi-pro. This experience taught him the importance of hard work, dedication, and teamwork. Steven now coaches basketball at the high school level. Thanks to his deep roots in the area, he has established relationships with various people in the local communities.
