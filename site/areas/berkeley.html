THE BERKELEY GUIDE

Intellectual Heritage & Timeless Craftsmanship

Home to the world's premier public research university, Berkeley is internationally recognized for its intellectual vitality, culinary pioneering in the Gourmet Ghetto, and iconic architecture by Julia Morgan, Bernard Maybeck, and John Hudson Thomas. From the grand estates of the Claremont Court to the cliffside sanctuaries of the Berkeley Hills, the city offers panoramic Bay and Golden Gate views.
