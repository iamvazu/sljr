The Estate Transition

Trading Dense Town Lots for Secluded Compound Living

While Palo Alto, Atherton, and Menlo Park represent the intellectual and financial core of Silicon Valley, their residential parcels are frequently confined by historic zoning, high density, and intensive property values exceeding $2,200 per square foot. Crossing the bay to Alamo unlocks rare Northern California privacy: multi-acre level parcels shaded by heritage oaks, private gated driveways, equestrian amenities, and panoramic views of Mount Diablo.

● Life in Palo Alto

  • Parcels: Compact 6,000 – 10,000 sq ft lots with close neighboring sightlines.
  • Price Point: $2,000 – $2,800+ per sq ft for updated single-family homes.
  • Outdoor Space: Small landscaped courtyards; limited room for pools, sports courts, or large ADUs.
  • Atmosphere: High-density, fast-paced collegiate tech energy with strict municipal tree and remodel restrictions.

● Life in Alamo

  • Parcels: 0.5 to 3+ acre private estates with expansive flat frontages and security gates.
  • Price Point: $950 – $1,400 per sq ft for world-class custom architectural residences.
  • Outdoor Space: Resort pools, championship pickleball/tennis courts, equestrian barns, detached guest houses, and private vineyards.
  • Atmosphere: Tranquil country-club exclusivity, dark night skies, canopy oak groves, and unhurried privacy.
Commute Blueprint

Executive Mobility & Cross-Bay Connectivity

Alamo’s position in the northern Diablo Valley provides multiple swift routes into Silicon Valley, San Francisco, and regional private aviation.

Highway 84 & Dumbarton Corridor

I-680 South connects seamlessly to Route 84 West across the Dumbarton Bridge directly into Menlo Park, Palo Alto, Stanford University, and the Meta campus in approximately 45 to 55 minutes off-peak.

Highway 92 / San Mateo Bridge

Connecting via I-580 West to Hwy 92 provides a direct path to Foster City, San Mateo, and northern Peninsula enterprise headquarters in under 40 minutes.

Private Aviation Terminals

Alamo residents frequently utilize Buchanan Field Airport (CCR) in Concord (12 minutes north) or Livermore Municipal Airport (LVK) (18 minutes south) for effortless private jet charter and regional executive travel.
