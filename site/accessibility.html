Accessibility Statement

Last updated: November 2026

Steven Lenz Jr. and Compass California II, Inc. are committed to providing a website that is accessible to the widest possible audience, regardless of technology or ability. We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA.

Measures We Take

Accessibility practices on this site include:

  • Semantic HTML landmarks and a logical heading structure.
  • Text alternatives for meaningful images.
  • Sufficient color contrast between text and backgrounds.
  • Keyboard operability for interactive elements, drawers, and modals.
  • Reduced-motion support that honors your operating system preference (prefers-reduced-motion).

Known Limitations

Some content is supplied by third parties, including MLS listing data and photographs, and may not fully meet accessibility standards. We work to remediate issues within our control as they are identified.

Feedback

If you experience difficulty accessing any part of this website, please contact us at (925) 998-9711 or steven.lenzjr@compass.com. Please describe the issue and any assistive technology you use. We aim to respond within 2–3 business days and to provide the information you need through an alternative method where possible.
