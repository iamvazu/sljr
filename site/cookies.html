1. What Are Cookies and Similar Technologies?

Cookies are small data files placed on your computer, tablet, or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used by online businesses and premier real estate platforms to make websites operate efficiently, enhance user navigation, and provide diagnostic reporting.

We may also utilize related technologies, including:

  • Web Beacons / Clear GIFs: Tiny graphic files containing unique identifiers that help us track whether a particular page or email notification was accessed.
  • HTML5 Local & Session Storage: Client-side browser storage mechanisms used to maintain responsive filter states, saved property IDs, and interactive viewing configurations.
  • Server Log Files: Automatic records of browser IP addresses, browser specifications, Internet Service Providers (ISPs), referring/exit URLs, date/time stamps, and clickstream sequences.

2. First-Party vs. Third-Party Cookies

First-Party Cookies: Set directly by stevenlenzjr.com to manage sessions, remember search filters, and store consent states.
Third-Party Cookies: Set by authorized partners, including web analytics providers (e.g. Google Analytics), mapping utilities, and digital marketing platforms to evaluate website effectiveness.
