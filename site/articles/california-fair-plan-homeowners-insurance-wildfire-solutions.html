Key Executive Takeaways

  • Properties in Westside Danville, Alamo foothills, and Oakland Hills often require hybrid coverage combining the California FAIR Plan with a Difference in Conditions (DIC) policy.
  • Securing insurance quotes during the initial 7-day contingency period prevents eleventh-hour mortgage underwriting freezes.
  • Defensible space landscaping, ember-resistant vents, and Class-A fire-rated roofing generate substantial carrier premium discounts.

With traditional carriers tightening underwriting across California, proactive insurance planning has become a critical milestone in luxury real estate transactions.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Difference in Conditions (DIC) policy in California?

A DIC policy wraps around the basic fire coverage of the California FAIR Plan to provide comprehensive liability, water damage, theft, and contents protection.
