The Post-NAR Settlement Era: Buyer Representation Agreements & Commission Clarity

Steven Lenz Jr. Compass · CA DRE# 01989811
2026-10-26 · 7 min read

Key Executive Takeaways

  • Written Buyer Representation Agreements (BRBC in California) must be executed prior to touring any residential property with a licensed agent.
  • Cooperative compensation is no longer advertised on the Multiple Listing Service (MLS), requiring direct bilateral negotiation between agents and sellers.
  • Sophisticated buyer representation provides indispensable value in due diligence, contract strategy, and closing negotiation that far outweighs advisory costs.

The historic National Association of REALTORS® settlement transformed real estate representation. Learn how modern buyer-broker agreements protect consumer interests and define fiduciary duties.

What the Buyer-Broker Representation Agreement (BRBC) Means for You

Rather than introducing friction, the new rules bring professional clarity. Homebuyers now formally interview and select their representation based on hyper-local expertise, transactional track record, and negotiation acumen. Sellers continue to routinely offer buyer broker concessions to attract the largest pool of qualified purchasing demand.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a seller still pay my buyer agent’s commission in California?

Yes. The vast majority of East Bay sellers continue to offer competitive seller concessions toward buyer broker representation to ensure their listings attract maximum qualified buyers.
