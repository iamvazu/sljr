California ADU Laws & SB 9: How Accessory Dwelling Units Add Tangible Value in Tri-Valley

Steven Lenz Jr. Compass · CA DRE# 01989811

Key Executive Takeaways

  • Detached accessory dwelling units (ADUs) up to 1,200 sq. ft. add an estimated $250,000 to $450,000+ in appraised residential market value.
  • ADUs provide flexible solutions for multi-generational living, live-in healthcare, executive home offices, or $2,500 to $3,800/mo in passive rental income.
  • Recent statutory reforms prohibit municipal impact fees on ADUs under 750 sq. ft. and mandate ministerial permit approvals within 60 days.

California’s progressive ADU legislation and SB 9 framework have turned suburban backyards into high-yield real estate assets across Pleasanton, Livermore, and Walnut Creek.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can HOAs in California prohibit ADUs?

No. Under California Government Code § 65852.2, CC&Rs or HOA bylaws that prohibit or unreasonably restrict the construction of ADUs or JADUs are void and unenforceable.
