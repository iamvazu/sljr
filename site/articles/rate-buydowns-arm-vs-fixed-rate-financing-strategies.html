Fixed-Rate vs. 7/1 ARM & Permanent 2-1 Buydowns: Tactical Mortgage Structuring

Steven Lenz Jr. Compass · CA DRE# 01989811
2026-11-09 · 6 min read

Key Executive Takeaways

  • A 2-1 temporary buydown lowers the borrower’s interest rate by 2% in Year 1 and 1% in Year 2, providing substantial cash-flow relief during the initial move-in phase.
  • 7/1 and 10/1 Hybrid Adjustable Rate Mortgages (ARMs) often trade 60 to 90 basis points below 30-year fixed loans with guaranteed stability throughout the initial term.
  • Structuring seller concessions toward mortgage rate points provides significantly greater monthly payment reduction than equivalent purchase price cuts.

In a transitional rate environment, savvy California buyers are bypassing standard 30-year fixed financing in favor of tactical 7/1 and 10/1 ARMs and seller-funded rate buydowns.

The Math of a 2-1 Temporary Buydown vs. Price Reduction

Consider a $2,000,000 purchase with 20% down ($1,600,000 loan) at a 6.50% base rate. Compare a $50,000 price drop against using that $50,000 as a seller-paid rate buydown:

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a buyer refinance if rates drop during a 2-1 buydown?

Yes! Any remaining unspent funds held in the lender’s buydown escrow account are credited directly against the principal payoff balance upon refinancing.
