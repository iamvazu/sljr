Key Executive Takeaways

  • San Ramon median single-family values reached $1,940,000, with Dougherty Valley and Gale Ranch leading transaction velocity.
  • Dublin’s transit-oriented luxury communities near BART and Fallon Gateway provide superior appreciation and rental yields.
  • Low average days on market (9 days) underscores strong demand from young professional families in Silicon Valley tech.

San Ramon and Dublin represent the growth engines of the Tri-Valley, combining state-of-the-art civic infrastructure with top-tier schools and modern luxury architecture.

The absorption rate across San Ramon and Dublin stands at an extraordinarily tight 84%, signaling an entrenched seller’s market where new listings are absorbed almost immediately upon release.

How do property tax rates compare in Dougherty Valley due to Mello-Roos?

Certain newer master-planned developments in Gale Ranch and Windemere have special assessments (Mello-Roos) that yield effective tax rates of 1.25% to 1.35%, which fund new community parks and award-winning schools.
