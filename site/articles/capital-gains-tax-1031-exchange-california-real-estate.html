Section 1031 Exchanges & Capital Gains Exemptions: Real Estate Wealth Preservation

Steven Lenz Jr. Compass · CA DRE# 01989811
2026-10-24 · 7 min read

Key Executive Takeaways

  • Section 121 allows married couples filing jointly to exclude up to $500,000 ($250,000 for singles) of capital gain upon selling a primary residence owned and lived in for 2 of the past 5 years.
  • Section 1031 exchanges permit real estate investors to defer 100% of federal and state capital gains and depreciation recapture taxes by reinvesting into like-kind property.
  • The 45-day replacement property identification window and 180-day closing rule require strict coordination with a Qualified Intermediary (QI).

In a high-bracket state like California, understanding IRC Section 121 primary home exclusions and Section 1031 tax-deferred exchanges is essential to preserving real estate equity.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I convert a primary residence into a 1031 investment property before selling?

Yes. Renting out your primary residence for a qualified period (typically 12 to 24 months) can establish investment character to qualify for a Section 1031 exchange.
