Frequently Asked Questions

What is the lifestyle difference between Pleasanton and Danville?

Pleasanton features a historic downtown with weekly farmer’s markets, fairgrounds, and parks. Danville offers a sophisticated village ambiance with boutique dining, Mount Diablo views, and equestrian heritage.
