Key Executive Takeaways

  • Always verbally verify wire instructions with your dedicated title escrow officer prior to wiring earnest money deposits (EMDs) or down payments.
  • Thorough review of the Seller Property Questionnaire (SPQ) and Real Estate Transfer Disclosure Statement (TDS) uncovers historical maintenance patterns.
  • Partnering with an experienced local advisor like Steven Lenz Jr. prevents costly rookie oversights and secures critical seller credits.

Navigating a multi-million-dollar real estate transaction in California requires disciplined financial coordination, comprehensive disclosure review, and airtight escrow security protocols.

The 7 Milestones of a Flawless California Escrow

From initial offer acceptance to final deed recording, here is the proven step-by-step roadmap to closing on your California residence:

MilestoneTimelineCritical Action Items
1. Mutual Contract RatificationDay 0Lock in contract terms, open escrow with reputable title company
2. Earnest Money Deposit (EMD)Within 24–72 HoursWire 3% deposit to title company (verbally verify wire details)
3. Due Diligence & DisclosuresDays 1 – 10Review inspection reports, preliminary title report, HOA docs, permit history
4. Appraisal & Loan ApprovalDays 7 – 17Lender appraisal completed, underwriter issues final loan commitment

Frequently Asked Questions

How does California protect buyers against wire fraud?

Escrow companies use encrypted verification portals and strict verbal confirmation protocols. Never rely on emailed bank wire instructions without calling the verified phone number of your title officer.
