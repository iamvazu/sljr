Smart Homes, Solar Mandates & Net-Zero Living: What Luxury Bay Area Buyers Expect

Steven Lenz Jr. Compass · CA DRE# 01989811
2026-10-22 · 6 min read

Key Executive Takeaways

  • Dual Tesla Powerwalls or Enphase battery storage systems paired with commercial-grade solar arrays provide off-grid security during Public Safety Power Shutoffs (PSPS).
  • Whole-home automation (Control4, Savant, Lutron Ketra) and motorized shading systems are baseline expectations for properties listed above $2.5M.
  • California Title 24 energy mandates ensure contemporary new builds achieve superior insulation, heat pump efficiency, and indoor air purification.

Modern luxury in Northern California is defined not merely by marble slabs and wine cellars, but by intelligent automation, energy resilience, and zero-carbon sustainability.

Substantially. Owned solar arrays add significant appraised value. Leased solar systems create contract assumption friction and appraisal complications that delay closing.
