Pleasanton Real Estate Market Report: Median Sale Prices, Comps & Neighborhood Analysis

Steven Lenz Jr. Compass · CA DRE# 01989811
2026-11-13 · 7 min read

Key Executive Takeaways

  • Pleasanton median single-family home prices reached $2,185,000, representing a 5.6% year-over-year increase.
  • Homes zoned for Foothill High School and Amador Valley High School commanded an average of $890 to $1,050 per square foot.
  • Average market time for turnkey listings stands at just 12 days, with 64% of sales closing above initial asking price.

Pleasanton continues to represent one of the most resilient and sought-after luxury suburban markets in Northern California, anchored by pristine neighborhoods and historic downtown charm.
