California Proposition 19 Complete Guide: Transferring Your Property Tax Base at Age 55+

Steven Lenz Jr. Compass · CA DRE# 01989811
2026-11-05 · 8 min read

Key Executive Takeaways

  • Eligible homeowners can transfer their factored base year value from their primary residence to any replacement residence located anywhere in California.
  • The transfer can be executed up to three times during a taxpayer’s lifetime (an increase from once under legacy Propositions 60 and 90).
  • If the replacement property costs more than the original home, only the incremental difference in market value is added to the transferred tax base.

California Proposition 19 represents the most profound real estate tax benefit in modern state history, empowering eligible homeowners aged 55 and older to downsize or relocate without facing crippling property tax re-assessments.

Critical 2-Year Replacement Window Rules

To qualify, the replacement home must be acquired or newly constructed within two years (before or after) of the sale of the original primary residence. Detailed documentation and timely filing with the county assessor’s office are non-negotiable.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I purchase a more expensive replacement home under Prop 19?

Yes. If your original home sold for $2.0M and your new home costs $2.3M, the $300,000 difference is added to your original tax base, still saving tens of thousands in annual taxes.
