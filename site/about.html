Steven Lenz Jr. is a full-time REALTOR® and Luxury Real Estate Advisor specializing in the East Bay’s prestigious Tri-Valley and Diablo Valley regions across Contra Costa and Alameda Counties.

As a lifelong Tri-Valley native raised in Pleasanton and an alumnus of Amador Valley High School, Steven offers clients a deep, foundational understanding of the local market landscape. His regional roots and extensive civic network provide buyers and sellers with a distinct competitive advantage in the fast-paced Bay Area market.

Whether representing an architectural luxury estate or securing a private enclave residence, Steven Lenz Jr. delivers a dedicated standard of service defined by preparation, fierce loyalty, and data-backed execution.
